TANAY RIZAL WEBSITE — UPDATED FROM YOUR SCREENSHOTS AND PDF

Files: index.html, style.css, script.js, assets/
Run by opening index.html in Chrome. Internet is required for Google Maps embeds and Google Fonts.

Includes the original locally extracted images, destination-specific descriptions and visitor planning notes, scroll reveal and hover animations, responsive navigation, destination category filters (including culture), and one embedded Google Map per featured and destination detail.

Maps are generated from the names/addresses included in the source content. Verify map results, current access conditions, schedules, and venue details before public release or travel.
